# PRD: Post 007 — Anomaly Detection & Dimensionality Reduction

**AI Engineering Lab Series | Era 1: Classic Machine Learning**

## Objective
Teach t-SNE, UMAP, and Isolation Forest using two novel datasets with rare anomalies.

## Datasets
| Dataset | Domain | Rows | Anomaly Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jet Engine Sensors | General (Aviation) | 4,650 | 3.2% |
| Silicon Thermal Hotspots | Post-Silicon Validation | 9,300 | 3.2% |

## Concepts Covered
t-SNE (perplexity, local structure), UMAP (global topology), Isolation Forest (contamination, anomaly scores), ROC-AUC for anomaly detection, t-SNE vs PCA vs UMAP comparison.
