\section{Project Overview}

I applied t-SNE, UMAP, and Isolation Forest for anomaly detection in two datasets. These methods effectively identified rare anomalies in jet engine and silicon thermal sensor data. Key results include successfully isolating anomalies and visualizing complex patterns.

\textbf{t-SNE:} Reduces dimensions while preserving local relationships.

\textbf{UMAP:} Offers faster dimensionality reduction while maintaining global structure.

\textbf{Isolation Forest:} Detects anomalies by randomly isolating outliers.
